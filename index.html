Data and Accounts

Clear this device’s data

Remove this account’s reading settings, local practice results, pending reports and saved inbox from this browser, plus GES Pasco’s downloaded files. Other accounts’ local records and your cloud account remain.

Cloud reading data can return after reopening. Unsynced local data will be lost. The book download is shared by accounts in this browser.

Delete account permanently

Remove your GES Pasco login, cloud reading position, preferences, bookmarks, inbox, submitted question reports and reviewer access. This browser’s local data and downloads will also be removed.

Shared study content and review audit records remain. Copies already saved on other devices must be cleared there. Deleting a Google-linked GES Pasco account does not delete your Google account.

Once deletion starts, it cannot be undone. If interrupted, return here to finish.
