Purpose

GES Pasco is a study and revision tool from AlatiphA Multimedia for promotion aptitude-test preparation. It is not an official examination service, and a practice score does not guarantee an examination result or promotion.

Study content and answers

The bundled past questions follow the book’s marked answers. Historical answers may need checking, and published reviewer corrections can differ from the original book. Use the explanations and sources, and report suspected errors through the practice answer review.

Published corrections apply to new practice sessions. Completed and unfinished saved attempts retain their original question and answer snapshots.

Your account

Keep your sign-in credentials secure and use an account you are authorized to access. Do not attempt to access another user’s private data or misuse reporting and reviewer tools.

Trial and subscriptions

The app’s seven-day trial counts from original account creation, including accounts created before subscriptions launched. After expiry, an approved subscription is required for in-app reading and new practice tests. Plans are GH₵20 for 1 month, GH₵50 for 3 months, GH₵80 for 6 months and GH₵100 for 12 months. MoMo transfers are manually verified before activation; a submitted transaction ID is not confirmation of receipt. Optional support payments do not purchase subscription access. Use the support contact for payment questions.

Offline use and saved work

Offline reading requires an active trial or approved subscription previously checked online, plus downloading the necessary files in each browser. Local-only practice results and pending reports can be lost if browser data is cleared or the browser removes stored files. Check readiness before relying on offline access.

Content and contributions

Respect rights in the app and its study materials. Do not assume that access to the reader grants permission to redistribute all book content. Submit reports, explanations and corrections you are entitled to share for review.

Deleting data or your account

Use Account → Data and Accounts for confirmed device cleanup or account deletion. Shared study content and review audit records remain after account deletion. The Privacy page explains the data retained and the difference between device cleanup and account deletion.
