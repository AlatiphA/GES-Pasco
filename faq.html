Frequently Asked Questions

Getting Started
GES Pasco helps Ghana Education Service workers prepare for promotion aptitude exams with past questions, an EPUB reader, practice tests and revision tools. Signed-in users can read the sample exams MoE/GES/DD/2020, MoE/GES/AD I/2020 and MoE/GES/AD II/2020, including their sections and related explanations. The three-day account-age trial uses this same sample access. An active subscription, administrator account or full-access grant unlocks every chapter and practice set.
Open Account → About → Install app. Use the browser’s install confirmation, or follow the device instructions shown. Installing the app does not restart your trial or download all offline files automatically.
Installing or updating the app and downloading books are separate steps. While connected, open Account → Reading and offline → Download / repair and wait for Ready for offline reading, which checks the sample book and reader files. Subscribers and full-access accounts should also open the full reader online until its text appears so the protected full book can be saved on that device. Open Practice tests online to load premium question sets for offline use. Stay signed in and prepare each device separately. Sample access continues after the three-day trial; a downloaded full book does not extend a paid subscription beyond its expiry.
Reading
Tap the menu button on the bottom left corner of the reader to open the sidebar, tap on contents tab and select the chapter (section).
Tap left or right on the reading area, or use the arrow buttons at the bottom of the screen.
Use A− and A+, or pinch with two fingers, to change text size. The A control offers font choices.
Tap the magnifying glass icon (🔍) in the bottom toolbar and type your search term. Results will appear as a list — tap any result to jump to that location.
Themes & Bookmarks
Use the palette button in the reader toolbar for Light, Dark, Sepia or Night. Account → Appearance separately controls menus and dialogs: System, Light or Dark.
Tap the bookmark icon (🔖) in the bottom toolbar. If you just reopened the app, wait briefly while it calculates page positions, then wait for Bookmark saved. The bookmark keeps the page you clicked, even if you move while calculation finishes. Its chapter name and percentage are saved with that passage.
Open the menu and select Bookmarks. Select an entry to return to its saved passage. The trash button beside an entry removes that bookmark. Bookmarks save on this device first and sync with the same account when connected.
Current reader and updates
An older version could use 0% before it finished calculating page positions. New bookmarks wait for a valid position. Older bookmarks already labelled 0% are not automatically rewritten: open one to check its saved passage and, if needed, save a replacement before removing the old entry. A genuine beginning-of-book bookmark can still show 0%.
Open Account → About → Check for updates while online. Wait for Update ready to install, then select Install update once. The app refreshes automatically. A slow book download does not block app installation; prepare books separately through Reading and offline. If an older tab cannot finish updating, use the app update page. Keep browser data so saved study work remains available.

The owner should check that the complete current Firestore rules are published and that the signed-in account has payment-administrator access. Then choose Show all users again. Reviewer access alone does not permit managing users.
Full practice papers
Quizzes now include all available sections, including True/False and written items adapted to answer choices. Most papers contain 75 questions; DD 2024, DD 2025 and AD I 2025 contain 70 numbered items. AD II 2025 has 56 available questions because the book lacks questions 31–36 and 51–58. These are not replaced with invented questions. An extra AD I 2025 item printed with duplicate number 43 is preserved in a separate set.
Open Account → Study tools → Practice tests, choose the paper, then set Number of questions to its displayed total. You can still choose fewer questions for a shorter session. Set the time limit to 0 for untimed practice. Older saved tests keep their original question count and score; bank updates apply to new attempts.
Troubleshooting
First check whether the app is asking you to renew or verify access. For a book-loading error, use Retry opening reader or, while online, Download / repair. Avoid clearing browser data as a routine repair; unsynced work can be lost.
The header now has a notification bell. Device cleanup is under Account → Data and Accounts and requires typing CLEAR. Export a backup and let cloud sync finish first. Cleanup removes local data and downloaded files; it does not restart a trial or delete your cloud account.
Close and reopen the app, then use Account → About → Check for updates. When ready, select Install update once; the app refreshes automatically. There is no separate Reload app step. For book errors, use Retry opening reader or Download / repair on the recovery screen. Account access can be refreshed with Check access after approval.
