Privacy

AlatiphA GES Pasco · Updated 29 October 2026

Account information

GES Pasco uses Firebase Authentication for email/password and Google sign-in. Your account can include your email, display name and a Google profile image. The app does not save your password in its local reading stores.

Reading and practice data

Reading position, bookmarks and reader preferences are saved on your device first and synchronized to your account when connected. Downloaded reader files are kept in this browser for offline use.

Completed practice results and question-level revision outcomes synchronize privately with your account when connected. Unfinished tests stay on the device where they started. The latest 20 results are shown; older cloud results are retained until account deletion. Published practice question sets can be cached for offline use.

Reports and notifications

Submitting a question report stores the question snapshot, source, issue, explanation you enter and your account identifier. Pending reports remain on the device until submitted. Reviewers can read and respond to submitted reports. Inbox messages and read markers are stored with your account; previously loaded messages may also be saved locally.

Notifications currently use the in-app inbox. Optional closed-app push notifications have not been activated.

Your choices

Account → Data and Accounts offers device cleanup and permanent account deletion. Device cleanup leaves your cloud account intact. Account deletion removes your GES Pasco login and personal cloud reader data, completed practice results, inbox, submitted reports and reviewer access. Shared study content, review audit records and the security record used to prevent recreation during deletion are retained. Local copies on other devices must be cleared there.

If account deletion is interrupted after it starts, sign in and return to Data and Accounts to finish. Clearing browser data removes local-only practice history, pending reports and downloads.

Service providers

GitHub Pages hosts the app, and Google Firebase provides authentication and cloud storage. Reader libraries, fonts and icons may be downloaded from third-party content delivery services used by the app. These services receive the network requests needed to deliver their resources. See Google’s Privacy Policy and GitHub’s Privacy Statement for their practices.
